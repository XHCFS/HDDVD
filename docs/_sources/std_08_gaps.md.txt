# S8. Gaps and single-specimen caveats

The honest boundary of this archival set. Standard Content rests on one disc, so this
sheet states plainly what is solid, what could be a Scenarist artifact, and what was
never decoded.

## 8.1 The single-specimen problem

Everything graded SINGLE was read from `RESERVOIR_DOGS` alone, with no patent RBP table
and no second disc to confirm it. Such a claim could be a property of Sonic Scenarist
SCA 4.2 rather than of the format. The VERIFIED claims are safer, because they either
match a patent RBP table (VMGI_MAT, VTSI_MAT) or close arithmetically or chain-validate
over many samples (TT_SRPT stride, cell chaining across 204 cells, TMAPT durations, the
40-VOBU DSI walk).

A second Standard Content disc would settle most SINGLE items at once. The most
promising lead is Sonic Scenarist sample projects, since authoring tools ship them and
the one specimen came from that toolchain. None was located in this work.

## 8.2 Solid (VERIFIED)

- Volume layer: UDF 2.50 with Metadata Partition; category detection ([§1](std_01_volume.md)).
- VMGI_MAT and VTSI_MAT byte layouts, including the two HD DVD-inserted VMGI fields and
  the shifted VTSI pointer block (patent and disc agree) ([§2](std_02_vmgi.md), [§3](std_03_vtsi.md)).
- VTS_TMAPT and VTS_PTT_SRPT (fully cross-validated) ([§3](std_03_vtsi.md)).
- VTS_ATRT table geometry: 11 entries of 926 bytes, arithmetic-closing ([§2.4](std_02_vmgi.md)).
- The 16-bit command rotation and the VM type dispatch ([§4](std_04_pgc_vm.md)).
- EVO container and the NV_PCK packet set (PCI, DSI, GCI) ([§5](std_05_evob_nv.md)).
- Time and sector seek via TMAPT and DSI ([§5](std_05_evob_nv.md), [§6](std_06_playback.md)).

## 8.3 Plausible but single-specimen (SINGLE)

- TT_SRPT 16-byte stride and field widths.
- VTS_PGCIT 12-byte SRP.
- The PGC header layout, the two palettes, and cell_playback at 28 bytes.
- The specific first-play and post-command decodes (`JumpTT 15`, `CallSS VTSM`).
- PCI and DSI general-information offsets (proven on one disc, later confirmed on
  Advanced).
- VMGM_PGCI_UT hierarchy and the menu PGC body layout (decoded on the specimen; the
  menu PGC's 28-byte cell stride closes) ([§2.3](std_02_vmgi.md)).
- VTS_ATR attribute-block interior (mirrors the VERIFIED VTSI_MAT attributes)
  ([§2.4](std_02_vmgi.md)).

## 8.4 Open (not decoded, or patent-only)

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| HDVTSM (VTS menu) `PGCI_UT` body | OPEN; the VMG menu `PGCI_UT` is decoded ([§2.3](std_02_vmgi.md)) and the VTS menu follows the same DVD-shaped hierarchy (patent [3] FIG.20) |

## 8.5 Provenance trail

This set distils the earlier working notes in `spec/clean/` (`00_PRIMER_dvd_to_hddvd`,
`01_VMGI_MAT_and_TT_SRPT`, `02_VTSI_MAT_PGC_and_commands`, `08_NV_PCK_PCI_DSI`,
`12_DISC_STRUCTURE`, `13_PLAYBACK`) into a single archival specification. Where a note
there disagrees with these sheets, these sheets win. The patent-figure reading behind
the Standard-Content structures is recorded in
``spec/clean/16b_WO_FIGURE_INDEX.md``.
